Legal Metrology Statutory Guide & Reference

Handbook on Packaged Commodities Rules 2011 and Inspector Verification Guidelines

Rule 6: Mandatory Declarations on Pre-Packaged Commodities

Every pre-packaged commodity must display the following unambiguous statutory declarations:

Rule 6(1)(a)
Name and Address of Manufacturer / Packer / Importer: Complete postal address with PIN code where packaging took place.
Rule 6(1)(b)
Generic or Common Name: Clear, conspicuous identity of commodity in package.
Rule 6(1)(c)
Net Quantity Declaration: Must use standard legal metric symbols (g, kg, ml, L, m, cm). Non-standard units (gms, kgs) violate Rule 13(2).
Rule 6(1)(d)
Date of Manufacture / Packing & Batch: Month and year (MM/YYYY) plus lot/batch number under Rule 6(1)(da).
Rule 6(1)(e)
Maximum Retail Price (MRP): Mandatory tax inclusion phrase: "MRP Rs. XX.XX incl. of all taxes" or "inclusive of all taxes".
Rule 6(1)(m)
Unit Sale Price (USP): Weight < 1kg → ₹/g; ≥ 1kg → ₹/kg; Vol < 1L → ₹/ml; ≥ 1L → ₹/L; Unit → ₹/unit. Exempt when quantity is exactly 1kg or 1L.
Rule 6(1)(n)
Consumer Care Information: Must provide person's name/designation, postal address, helpline phone, and email address.

Enforcement & Penalties

Section 36(1) of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009

Pre-packaged commodities not conforming to statutory declarations are subject to penalties: First offence up to ₹25,000; second up to ₹50,000; subsequent offences up to ₹1,00,000 or imprisonment up to 1 year.

Inspector Best Practices

  • Upload multiple clear images (front, back, side panels) for 100% declaration coverage.
  • Ensure lighting is even without glare over shiny foil or plastic surfaces.
  • Verify that calculated Unit Sale Price matches the printed USP rate.
  • Use the "Review & Edit" modal to rectify any OCR ambiguity before issuing report.
